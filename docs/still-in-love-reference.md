[Umamusume wiki page](https://umamusu.wiki/Still_in_Love), read on
2026-10-26. Anything not from there is marked.

## Character

- **Tagline:** "I still love you". Emoji: a red heart.
- **On the surface:** quiet and easily overlooked. She practises smiling in a mirror and keeps a diary of something she wants to do each day.
- **Underneath:** an alter ego that wakes at the full moon, with vampire traits. She dislikes sunlight, bites her trainer in her story, and dreams of being a vampire princess. Admire Groove calls her "her prey".
- **Likes:** bobbin lace, making red fruit jam, sweets, coffee, tea (iced lemon tea), cafés, historical novels, golf, her diary.
- **Dislikes:** large mirrors, strong-smelling food, full moons, direct sunlight, hot weather, crowds.
- **Red thread:** her Complete Triple Tiara event refers to the red thread of fate (akai ito).
- **Racehorse:** 2003 Triple Tiara winner, the second ever. She ran 5th as favourite in the Rose Stakes.

**Not from the wiki:** the screen getting redder the longer you stay with her. Fwoxie described this; I have not checked it in the game.

## How the theme uses it

| Reference | In the scene |
| --- | --- |
| Red and blue image colours | the palette: wine-dark cards, her blue as the accent; red is kept for "behind" |
| Red eyes | her eyes, glowing more as she turns (until her chibi exists) |
| Full moon dislike, alter ego | the blood moon in the "next rank" window; the calm window has a crescent |
| Bobbin lace | the lace trim along the bottom of the band |
| Red fruit jam | the jam jars on the side table |
| Large mirrors | the mirror on the table is draped with cloth |
| Tagline | her line in the speech bubble |

**Not used yet:** the veil and heart ring, gold hearts, blue diamonds, ribbons, her diary, coffee or tea, and the red thread. A red thread from her to the cards needs her chibi to start from; on bare eyes it looked like a tail.
