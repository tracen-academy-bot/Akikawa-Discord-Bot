[Umamusume wiki page](https://umamusu.wiki/Still_in_Love), its
[game page for her card](https://umamusu.wiki/Game:Still_in_Love_(%E7%B7%8B%E6%83%B3%E3%81%AE%E8%AA%93%E8%A1%A3))
and her [GameTora profile](https://gametora.com/umamusume/characters/still-in-love),
all read on 2026-10-26. Anything not from there is marked.

## Character

- **Tagline:** "I still love you". Emoji: a red heart.
- **Profile line (GameTora):** "Torn between reason and instinct. A bewitching lady with an unquenched thirst."
- **Card title:** 緋想の誓衣. The wiki gives no translation; 緋 means scarlet.
- **Unique skill:** スカーレットリリィの高揚 ("Scarlet Lily"; the wiki gives no English name). Other skills (JP only): 衝動 (impulse), 内なる獣 (the beast within), 想い焦がれて (burning with longing), 誰にも告げぬ誓い (a vow told to no one). These translations are mine.
- **Quotes (GameTora):** "There is nothing in particular worth saying about me..." and "Only, yes... things that must remain secret...".
- **Ears:** point unwaveringly at her trainer.
- **On the surface:** quiet and easily overlooked. She practises smiling in a mirror and keeps a diary of something she wants to do each day.
- **Underneath:** an alter ego that wakes at the full moon, with vampire traits. She dislikes sunlight, bites her trainer in her story, and dreams of being a vampire princess. Admire Groove calls her "her prey".
- **Likes:** bobbin lace, making red fruit jam, sweets, coffee, tea (iced lemon tea), cafés, historical novels, golf, her diary.
- **Dislikes:** large mirrors, strong-smelling food, full moons, direct sunlight, hot weather, crowds.
- **Red thread:** her Complete Triple Tiara event refers to the red thread of fate (akai ito).
- **Racehorse:** 2003 Triple Tiara winner, the second ever. She ran 5th as favourite in the Rose Stakes.

**The red screen:** the wiki's trivia says her career has increasingly unsettling visual effects, with a motion-sickness warning and an option to turn them off. It does not describe the effects. "Redder the longer you stay" is Fwoxie's description.

## How the theme uses it

| Reference | In the scene |
| --- | --- |
| Red and blue image colours | the palette: wine-dark cards, her blue as the accent; red is kept for "behind" |
| Red eyes | her eyes, glowing more as she turns (until her chibi exists) |
| Full moon dislike, alter ego | the blood moon in the "next rank" window; the calm window has a crescent |
| Bobbin lace | the lace trim along the bottom of the band |
| Red fruit jam | the jam jars on the side table |
| Large mirrors | the mirror on the table is draped with cloth |
| Tagline | her line in the speech bubble |
| Scarlet Lily skill | a vase of red spider lilies on the side table |
| "Unquenched thirst", vampire | a glass of red wine on the table, spilling over at her worst |
| Ribbons, gold hearts | white ribbon tie-backs with gold hearts on the curtains; gold heart charms on the lace |
| Blue diamond | one blue diamond at the centre of the lace |
| Roses | not canon (only the Rose Stakes); one lies on the calm window's ledge |

**Not used yet:** the veil and heart ring, her diary, coffee or tea, the red thread, and the skill names (possible speech-bubble lines). A red thread from her to the cards needs her chibi to start from; on bare eyes it looked like a tail.
