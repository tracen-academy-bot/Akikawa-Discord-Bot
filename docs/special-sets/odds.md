# Special odds

Generated by `scripts/special-odds-table.ts` from the constants in `src/lib/timer/openers.ts`. Do not edit by hand.

Base special chance: **10%** a ping. Boosts add to it; the total then goes through the soft caps.

## Soft caps

Up to 20% counts in full. Raw chance above 20% counts at half. Raw chance above 30% counts at a quarter. The effective chance never passes 30%. The base slice is never cut; the boosts shrink in proportion.

| Raw total | Effective |
| --- | --- |
| 10% | 10% |
| 20% | 20% |
| 25% | 22.5% |
| 30% | 25% |
| 35% | 26.3% |
| 40% | 27.5% |
| 50% | 30% |
| 60% | 30% |
