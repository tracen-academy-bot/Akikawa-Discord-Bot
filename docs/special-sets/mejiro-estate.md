# Special set: The Mejiro Estate

Status: in code (`src/lib/timer/openers.ts`, set id `mejiro-estate`). This file is the source text; keep the two in step.

**Live again (owner, 2026-11-15).** It was paused on 2026-11-10 (no new finds) while the lines were redesigned; it resumed with the new lines, Ramonu's Remedial Rehearsal and the first wave of guests. Finds made before the pause (Grandmother, once) were kept.

- **Premise:** the trainer is invited to a gathering at the Mejiro Estate; each line is one scene.
- **Style:** fancy, no hashtags (a guest like Helios may keep hers; Palmer's one #SweetieSecrecies is her imitating Helios).
- **Unlock:** the Set 1 line "Mejiro Family" (`set1.mejiro`).
- **Stages** (`Special.after`, never shown on the Specials page, which just says "???"):
  1. Grandmother's welcome comes first: any roll on a Mejiro line she has not unlocked yet gives her line instead (`redirectOpener`), so she takes the whole set's share (9x) until found.
  2. The seven Mejiro open after it, in any order.
  3. The Family Photograph opens after all eight; until then a roll on it gives an unmet family member.
  4. Guests (to come) open after the photograph; until then a roll on one redirects the same way.

## Outcomes (code: `RAMONU_OUTCOMES`, `REHEARSAL_OUTCOMES`, `REHEARSAL_FORCED`)

The footer goes under the line in the ping and its DM copy, never in the
read view. The odds are never shown.

- **Ramonu**, each time: 75% a lesson (the Rehearsal is scheduled on a run
  picked at random from the next 25, and comes up no other way; not while
  one is already pending or a losing streak runs); 25% footwork (+5% any
  Mejiro line for 50 runs).
- **No Ramonu until the lesson is passed** (owner, 2026-11-15): while the
  Rehearsal is scheduled, guaranteed, or a losing streak runs, Ramonu's own
  line is out of every pool (`Special.heldUntilPassed`); a win or the
  forced success brings her back.
- **Outcome boosts do not stack** (owner, 2026-11-15): the same one again
  tops it back up to its full runs, never past them.
- **Rehearsal**, 50/50: a win (+3% any Mejiro line for 25 runs, the losing
  streak back to 0) or a loss (one of the tail, the dress, the feet, never
  one already used in the streak; the trainer's next ping is the Rehearsal
  again).
- **Forced success** after three losses in a row: the line, then the
  paragraph, then `-# You obtained @Ramonu's Second Love.` (the role from
  `/timer rewards second_love_role`), or `-# Ramonu's Second Love, still.`
  if they had it. No role set, or no permission: the footer is left out.
- The footer texts are in `docs/special-sets/mejiro-estate-redesign.md`
  (sections 8 and 8b) and in code.

## Guests (code: `Special.guest`, `GUEST_SLICE`, `guestSlice`)

Owner's design, 2026-11-15. Five visitors in the first wave; multi-voice
dialogue is for guests only. Gold Ship and Teio use the owner's final copy
(2026-11-15), which replaces the redesign doc's drafts.

- Guests belong to this set and are marked `guest: true` (and `extra:
  true`, so every exclusion an extra has applies): never in the base pool
  (`rarePoolFor`), a set-wide boost, the unlock bonus, or the "any Mejiro
  line" outcome boosts.
- They exist for a trainer only once they have found **The Family
  Photograph** (`mejiro.photo`; each guest has `after: ['mejiro.photo']`).
- Their own slice, an extra chance in `extraChances`, counted toward the
  soft caps like the others: 4% x (guests the trainer has not found / 5).
  So 4%, 3.2% after one, 0 (no slice) once all five are found. It draws from
  all five, so repeats can come up while it is open.
- Content, not a boost: Boosts off does not pause it. It gives nothing
  while the set is paused, off, or locked for the trainer. Boosts view:
  "The Mejiro Estate: guests are dropping by."
- Never in set completion or any "N of M" count; they score find points
  like any special (15 new, 3 repeat).
- Specials page: after the 9 lines and the Rehearsal, as "**Guest.** ???"
  until found, then by name, readable from the read menu.
